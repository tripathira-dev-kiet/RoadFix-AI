How RoadFix AI Works

Streamlined civic issue lifecycle from detection to verified repair.

1

Snap & Geotag

Citizen captures road hazard photo; HTML5 GPS logs precise coordinates.

2

AI Hazard Rating

Vision AI classifies hazard (Pothole, Drainage, Streetlight) & assigns severity score.

3

Municipal Dispatch

Admin reviews assessment and dispatches work order to the relevant department.

4

On-Site Field Repair

Field crew repairs hazard, rolls bitumen, and uploads post-repair photo proof.

5

Citizen Review

Citizen verifies resolution photo proof and rates repair satisfaction.
